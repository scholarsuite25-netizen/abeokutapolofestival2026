Heritage, Nobility & Lineage

A Century of Royal Patronage in the Gateway State

Since the inception of the Abeokuta Polo Club in 1928, the traditional thrones of Egbaland have offered unwavering stewardship to equestrian excellence. The Royal Cup marks the historical convergence where century-old traditional courts welcome visiting international players, celebrated captains, and distinguished global patrons.

Guest Advisory & Decorum

Royal Protocol, Dress Code & Etiquette Guide

In keeping with the solemn royal presence of His Royal Majesty and the sovereign monarchs of the Southwest, attendees are requested to observe traditional and modern dress standards.

styler Traditional Regal Attire
Aso Oke & Grand Agbada

Egba royal cloths, hand-woven Aso Oke, rich Agbada sets with traditional Fila caps and royal beads. Royal delegations are encouraged to display ancestral court regalia with full pride.

Recommended tones: Royal forest green, rich champagne gold, and heritage cream.
apparel Contemporary Polo Chic
High-Society Lounge & Millinery

Crisp tailored linen suits, Panama hats, floral garden daywear, and statement fascinators. Flat or wedge heels are highly recommended for the halftime divot stomping on turf.

Footwear note: Stiletto heels are prohibited on the playing turf.

Dais Etiquette & Timings

Guests must be seated by 13:30 WAT before the entrance of the Royal Procession. Flashes and candid photography within 10 meters of the Royal Dais are restricted to accredited royal palace archivists.

Armed security details must remain at the designated VIP security perimeter.
