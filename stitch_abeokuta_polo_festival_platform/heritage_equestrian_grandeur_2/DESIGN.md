## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the distinction of high-goal equestrian polo seamlessly fused with ancestral Yoruba royalty. Rooted in the ceremonial dignity of the Egbaland royal court and His Royal Majesty the Alake of Egbaland, the aesthetic pairs aristocratic sporting excellence with Nigerian cultural heritage.

The narrative architecture targets high-net-worth patrons, multinational conglomerate executives, royal fathers, visiting diaspora elites, and sovereign partners. The emotional tone evokes absolute exclusivity, cultural pride, uncompromised pedigree, and sovereign celebration.

The visual style unites **Aristocratic Heritage Editorial** with **Regal Sovereign Precision**:
- **Canvas & Form**: Vast, sunlit warm ivory canvases evocative of bespoke embossed invitations, private polo club verandas, and luxury parchment.
- **Tonal Anchor**: Deep royal forest emerald green (`#062d1d`) establishes structural prestige, recalling field turf, British racing green lineage, and ceremonial velour robes.
- **Metallic Hierarchy**: Champagne gold and polished royal brass serve strictly as badges of honour, heraldic crest trims, luxury sponsorship tiers, and hairline accents.
- **Typographic Dialectic**: Editorial high-contrast serifs convey lineage and sovereign weight, contrasted against disciplined, modernist geometric sans-serifs that anchor commercial tiers, match brackets, and investment schedules.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the royal enclosure of the Abeokuta Polo Club at Kobape, reflecting the meeting of the King of Games with the royal institution of Egbaland.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#062d1d` - Royal Emerald)**: The foundational brand anchor. Governs sovereign displays, primary call-to-action fills, VIP hospitality backdrops, header banners, and prestigious nocturnal moments (Bonfire Night & Fireworks).
- **Secondary (`#cfa758` - Champagne Gold)**: The mark of prestige. Applied to ceremonial crests, metallic trims, trophy naming indicators, active navigation tab rules, and primary partnership status badges.
- **Tertiary (`#795910` - Royal Brass)**: Deep, authoritative bronze-gold. Applied to secondary interactive triggers, borders on dark cards, and formal subheadings.
- **Neutral (`#faf9f5` - Warm Ivory)**: The natural, high-grade linen canvas base. Replaces stark clinical whites to evoke bespoke bond paper, ceremonial marquees, and sun-drenched field pavilions.

### Functional Rules
- Deep emerald (`#062d1d`) and charcoal (`#111827`) serve as contrast anchors; avoid flat black (`#000000`).
- Champagne gold (`#cfa758`) must remain an architectural accent, keyline border, or badge element—never used for dense paragraph text.
- Sponsorship tiers are codified by metallic values: Title Partner (`#d4af37`), Presenting Partner (`#cfa758`), Royal Cup (`#795910`), Platinum Property (`#8a9ea7`), and Gold Property (`#dfbe75`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an aristocratic cadence between ceremonial tradition and commercial authority.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical majesty and royal protocol. Applied to overarching narratives (*"Where Heritage Meets Excellence"*), trophy naming rights (*"The Royal Cup"*), signature moments, and royal salutations. Italicize key phrases selectively for formal emphasis.
- **Interface & Operational Hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers structured clarity for commercial investment tiers, venue schedules, financial disclosures (e.g., *₦300M festival allocation*), and hospitality booking engines.
- **Luxury Label & Metatag Protocol**: All metadata tags, cup denominations, dates, and sponsorship tiers must use `label-sm` or `label-md` set in uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an elite print folio: expansive margins, unhurried negative space, and disciplined column structures.

- **Grid Structure**: Responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop, shifting to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Section Pacing**: Major narrative transitions (e.g., Royal Court Protocol to Partnership Packages) require large breathing room (`space-3xl` / `6rem`) on desktop.
- **Editorial Reading Constraint**: Long-form passages, curatorial statements, and royal proclamations must not exceed a maximum width of `680px` to maintain optimal typographic cadence.
- **Grid Alignment by Content**:
  - *Signature Moments*: 3-column desktop layout (`4` columns each) cascading to single-column swipe cards on mobile.
  - *Sponsorship Matrix*: Full 12-column comparative data table on desktop; responsive nested card stack with gold keylines on mobile.
  - *Hospitality Options*: 3-column asymmetric layout showcasing Royal Patrons' Circle, Hospitality Chalets, and Public/VIP passes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile craftsmanship, subtle tonal stacking, and hairline borders rather than synthetic drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering**:
  - *Base Enclosure*: Warm Ivory (`#faf9f5`).
  - *Intermediate Tier (Cards & Panels)*: Cream Surface (`#f7f5ed`) framed with 1px `border-warm` (`#e5e0d3`).
  - *Sovereign Vault (VIP & Night Moments)*: Deep Royal Emerald (`#062d1d`) with champagne gold hairline borders (`#cfa758` at 30% opacity).
- **Hairline Definition**: Structural demarcation relies on 1px precision keylines using `border-warm` (`#e5e0d3`) on light surfaces or subtle champagne gold (`#dfbe75` at 25%) on dark containers.
- **Ambient Diffusion**: For modal dialogs, elevated partner suites, and floating luxury controls, use warm-tinted diffused shadows: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(6, 45, 29, 0.08)`. Never employ desaturated or cool gray shadows.
- **Tactile Sheen**: Premium property badges (Title Partner ₦75M, Royal Cup ₦25M) utilize a micro-gradient sheen running 135 degrees from polished gold (`#dfbe75`) to champagne brass (`#cfa758`).

## Shapes

The system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** corner geometry (0.25rem / 4px base radius), honoring bespoke stationery and architectural clubhouse woodwork.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Buttons, input fields, accordion panels, and structured table modules retain crisp 4px corners.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Signature moment cards, hospitality chalet packages, and tier showcase containers.
- **Capsule Pill (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status indicators, sponsorship slot badges (e.g., `1 SLOT ONLY`), category tags, and royal insignia frames.
- **Sharp (`0px`)**: Full-bleed equestrian photography frames, structural horizontal rules, and budget breakdown matrix dividers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Sovereign Action**: Deep Royal Emerald (`#062d1d`) fill, warm ivory typography (`#faf9f5`), 1px champagne gold border (`#cfa758`), `rounded` (4px). Label in uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.06em`). Hover: Transitions to `#002e1d` with subtle gold luminescence.
- **Secondary Champagne Outline**: 1.5px champagne gold keyline (`#cfa758`), transparent background, charcoal text (`#111827`) or warm ivory on dark surfaces. Hover: 8% champagne wash.
- **Text Link**: Charcoal with a persistent 1px hairline underline in `#cfa758`, expanding outward on hover.

### Badges & Sponsorship Chips
- **Slot Availability Badges**: Capsule-shaped (`9999px`), background in `#f7f5ed`, 1px border in `#dcd5c2`, typography in `label-sm`. For exclusive single-issue slots (e.g., Title Partner, Royal Cup), use a champagne gold background with deep emerald text.
- **Tier Micro-Badges**: Crisp uppercase labels displaying investment values (e.g., `₦75M`, `₦40M`, `₦25M`, `₦15M`, `₦8M`) formatted in `label-sm`.

### Signature Moment Cards
- Dedicated modules for the six exclusive properties: *The Royal Cup*, *All-Stars Game*, *Polo Queens Match*, *Bonfire Night & Fireworks*, *Kings Procession*, and *VIP Pavilion & Style Enclosure*.
- Structured with a `cream-surface` (`#f7f5ed`) background, 1px `border-warm` hairline, day/time label in gold, `headline-sm` title, single-slot badge, and a bold investment denominator.
- *Bonfire Night* and *Kings Procession* may feature an inverted Royal Emerald (`#062d1d`) background with ivory typography and champagne accents.

### Partnership Package Cards & Comparison Table
- Distinct tiers featuring structured layout blocks: Package Name, Investment (₦), Slot Quota, Headline Rights, and VIP Pass Allocations (from Title Partner's 40 passes to Official Category's 2 passes).
- Prominent callout cards for *Title Partner (₦75M)* and *The Royal Cup (₦25M)*, highlighting category exclusivity and the Alake of Egbaland royal presentation ceremony.

### Royal Hospitality & Chalet Showcase
- Dedicated 3-column layout highlighting *Royal Patrons' Circle (₦500,000)*, *Private Hospitality Chalet (₦1,500,000)*, and *Public/VIP Day Passes (from ₦5,000)*.
- Features tailored amenity lists: Patrons' Wall inclusion, field-side private chalet siting, dedicated guest passes, and royal presentation seating.

### Financial Allocation & Governance Matrix
- Transparent budget disclosure module tracking the ₦300M festival expenditure (Polo Operations ₦66M, Venue & Infrastructure ₦60M, Culture & Fireworks ₦45M, Royal Protocol ₦36M, etc.).
- Formatted with clean horizontal hairline dividers, monospace-aligned currency values, and subtle progress indicators.

### Roadmap & Activation Steps
- Chronological timeline indicators for both partnership milestones (31 Oct through 18–20 Dec) and commercial activation steps (Brief, Proposal, Agreement, Activation).
- Marked with numbered brass step indicators and champagne connecting keylines.
