Divot Stomping Footwear Protocol

Between chukkers, guests are invited onto the pitch for the traditional divot stomp. Flat soled leather shoes, loafers, or sturdy block wedges are highly recommended over stiletto heels to safeguard the hallowed turf.

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Guest Protocol & Verification Guidance

Comprehensive details regarding arrival, gate credentials, vehicular access, and tournament grounds etiquette.

Tournament grounds open daily at 10:00 AM for General and VIP Enclosures. The Opening Chukker commences at 1:30 PM, with the premier cups (including the Royal Cup matches) played between 3:30 PM and 6:00 PM. Evening hospitality, live musical performances at the Aristos Pavilion, and the Bonfire Grounds continue until 11:00 PM nightly.
Chalet reservation holders and Royal Patrons receive dedicated vehicular windshield passes granting access via the Kobape VIP Gate. Dedicated chauffeur holding zones with refreshments are provided. For guests arriving via chartered helicopter, coordinate clearance with our secretariat at least 48 hours prior for helipad slot reservation at the adjacent Ogun State Aviation hub.
Children accompanied by adults are welcomed within the General Enclosure and Private Chalets. However, due to service of alcoholic beverages and high decibel acoustic performances, the Aristos Style Pavilion and Royal Sunset Banquet are strictly reserved for guests aged 18 and older. Children must be supervised at all times along the turf boundaries.
General Passes and VIP Day Passes can be acquired online via debit card or instant bank transfer, issuing an instant cryptographic QR-code badge to your smartphone. For Corporate Chalets and Royal Patrons' Circles, formal pro-forma invoices with dedicated corporate bank details are issued by Abeokuta Polo Club. Accreditation badges are delivered physically via courier or collected at the Secretariat Desk.
