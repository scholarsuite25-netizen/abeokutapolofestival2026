Ground Regulations & Passholder Inquiries

Please review tournament protocol prior to arriving at the Kobape Polo Grounds.

General lawn admission passes are flexible, but Sovereign VIP Passes and Patrons' Circle credentials are cryptographically keyed to the verified registrant’s identification. If you are registering on behalf of a state dignitary or spouse, please ensure their exact legal name is entered into the accreditation portal before 15 December 2026.
Sovereign VIP and Royal Patron passholders have designated priority valet parking immediately adjacent to Gate 1 and Gate 2 along the Abeokuta–Sagamu Expressway. General admission ticketholders have access to the secure Kobape North Meadow parking lot with complimentary shuttle transfers every 10 minutes to the tournament grounds.
Children are warmly welcomed at the festival. Families are encouraged to enjoy the open spectator lawns and Ogun Cultural Village. The Aristos Grandstand and Sovereign Dais are reserved for guests aged 14 and above to ensure an undisturbed viewing ambiance during the high-goal chukkers.
Polo matches proceed in sunny conditions and light showers, as the Kobape turf is professionally drained. In the unlikely event of extreme squalls, chukkers will be temporarily paused and guests hosted in the sheltered Royal Pavilions. Passholders receive real-time notifications via their registered pass number.
