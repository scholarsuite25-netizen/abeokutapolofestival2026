# Abeokuta Polo Festival 2026

Where Heritage Meets Excellence.

Dates
    18–20 December 2026

Venue
    Abeokuta Polo Club, Abeokuta Shagamu Express Way, Kobape Abeokuta

Exact daily timings are pending. Only the date range is shown, and no countdown is displayed.

## Five pillars

The festival is built on five principal pillars.

  * ### Sport

High-quality polo competition featuring distinguished players and teams.

  * ### Heritage

The rich history, identity and cultural heritage of Abeokuta and Ogun State.

  * ### Tourism

An attractive destination experience for visitors from Nigeria and beyond.

  * ### Lifestyle

Lifestyle programming is pending. Details to be announced.

  * ### Brand Experience

Meaningful opportunities for sponsors to engage audiences through premium physical and digital experiences.




## Programme highlights

Three sporting concepts, shown as an unordered set. The running order is pending and none of them is a confirmed booking.

  * ### The Royal Cup

Signature heritage polo event, presented in honour of His Royal Majesty, the Alake and Paramount Ruler of Egbaland.

  * ### Polo Queens Match

An all-female polo showcase celebrating the elegance, skill, strength and excellence of women in polo, while creating a distinctive platform for female participation and leadership in the sport.

  * ### All-Stars Game

The King of Games. A special high-profile match featuring selected star players and leading polo talents, providing the festival's major sporting spectacle and a fitting finale to the competitive programme.




[See the full programme](/en/programme)

## Programme preview

Only the date range 18–20 December 2026 is confirmed. Day-by-day timings, match times, teams and players are not announced. Details to be announced.

## Experiences

Hospitality, culture and celebration across the three days.

  * ### Corporate hospitality

Hospitality guests include clients, investors, business associates, distributors, senior executives, government stakeholders, strategic partners and VIP guests. Packages can include reserved seating, VIP access, branded lounges, refreshments, entertainment and curated guest experiences.

  * ### Culture and performance

The Kings Procession, the Beriora / Beriola performance and ARISTOS BAND are programme concepts. None of them is a confirmed booking.

  * ### Celebration

Bonfire Night, with fireworks, ushers in the beginning of the Christmas and New Year celebrations in Abeokuta.
