## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the distinction of high-goal polo blended with deep historical royalty. The aesthetic balances time-honored heritage with modern editorial sophistication. The UI targets affluent sports patrons, cultural dignitaries, corporate sponsors, and luxury travelers seeking an exclusive tournament experience.

The design movement combines **Minimalist High-End Editorial** with **Tactile Heritage Accents**:
- Vast, warm ivory canvases evoke bespoke stationery and sunlit club pavilions.
- Deep royal emerald green serves as the foundational anchor, establishing solemnity, authority, and athletic heritage.
- Warm champagne gold is employed with precision for crests, micro-badges, and refined keyline accents, avoiding garishness.
- Typography honors print tradition: high-contrast serifs convey lineage and ceremonial weight, paired with razor-sharp geometric sans-serifs for legibility across digital schedules and ticketing flows.
- Visual rhythm relies on balanced margins, delicate gold-tinged dividers, and restrained spatial pacing rather than dense utility.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in aristocratic natural tones and regal finishes:

- **Primary (`#062d1d`)**: Deep British Racing / Royal Emerald Green. Used for primary interactive triggers, primary navigation bars, dark thematic sections (VIP, Evening Galas), and deep brand anchors.
- **Secondary (`#cfa758`)**: Subtle Champagne Gold. Reserved for insignia badges, awards highlights, primary active states, tournament trophy badges, and delicate metallic borders.
- **Tertiary (`#083c28`)**: Forest Accent Green. Used for secondary tier banners, high-contrast badges within cream spaces, and subtle hover tints.
- **Neutral (`#fcfbf7`)**: Warm Ivory Canvas. Forms the standard light mode background, offering a softer, more luxurious editorial reading plane than harsh pure white.
- **Named Colors**:
  - `gold-light` (`#dfbe75`): Subtle borders and glowing micro-highlights against dark surfaces.
  - `gold-metallic` (`#d4af37`): Trophy accents, cup iconography, and luxury ribbon badges.
  - `cream-surface` (`#f7f5ed`): Surface container fills for cards, input backgrounds, and accordion panels.
  - `charcoal-text` (`#111827`): Deep slate for primary body copy, headlines on light mode, and contrast text.
  - `border-warm` (`#e5e0d3`) & `border-refined` (`#dcd5c2`): Hairline rules, card frames, and luxury structural dividers.

Avoid heavy solid black fills. Let dark emerald green serve as the deepest tonal anchor. Gold must remain an accent or trim—never used for long-form reading text.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a tension between classical prestige and contemporary functional clarity.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Applied to all brand statements, tournament cup titles, narrative subheadings, and quote callouts. Use italic styling sparingly for cultural epigrams and ceremonial titles (e.g., *“Where Heritage Meets Excellence”*).
- **Functional Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Applied to match schedules, ticketing tiers, long-form editorial descriptions, metadata, and controls. Its clean geometric precision counterbalances the ornate serif headers.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: Uppercase treatments with expanded letter spacing (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) are mandatory for category chips, date stamps, VIP tier tags, and cup standings to create an archival, luxury pass aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous margins that honor editorial luxury print layouts.

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 4rem margins. Structural sections utilize wide breathing room (`space-3xl`) between narrative chapters and cup showcases.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 2rem margins. Complex grids (such as the Five Strategic Pillars) shift to 2-column or 3-column alternating card layouts.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins. Tournament cups and schedules collapse into horizontal swipe rails or single-column stacks with consistent `space-lg` card separators.

Vertical flow must favor asymmetrical white space to celebrate imagery of horses, polo mallets, and VIP spectators. Keep content widths constrained for long-form passages (maximum 680px) to maintain editorial rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy dropshadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Crisp Keylines**, and **Subtle Warm Ambient Casts**:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - Base: Warm Ivory (`#fcfbf7`).
  - Elevated Surface: Cream Tier (`#f7f5ed`) framed with 1px hairline borders (`#e5e0d3`).
  - Dark Immersion Surface: Royal Emerald (`#062d1d`) with champagne gold border highlights (`#dfbe75` at 20% opacity).
- **Hairline Framing**: Luxury is communicated through physical framing. Cards, modals, and dropdowns employ crisp, 1px rules in `border-warm` or `border-refined`.
- **Ambient Shadows**: When elevation is required for floating navigation or modals, use ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadows: `0 16px 36px -10px rgba(6, 45, 29, 0.08)`. Never use pure gray or black shadow offsets.
- **Micro-Gleam**: Elevated luxury badges and VIP tier cards may use a faint linear gradient overlay from top-left to bottom-right (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` fading to transparent) to create the feel of heavy cardstock or polished brass.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness posture (0.25rem base radius). 

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, accordion panels, and pillar cards) carry subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) corner rounding. This preserves crisp, tailored borders reminiscent of architectural clubhouses and engraved invitations.
- Card containers and tournament modules use `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`).
- Exceptions:
  - **Pill Shape (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for metadata chips, date tags, status badges, and circular crest medallions.
  - **Sharp (`0px`)**: Used for fine structural divider lines, full-bleed imagery picture frames, and secondary table rules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action**: Deep Royal Emerald (`#062d1d`) fill, high-contrast ivory text (`#fcfbf7`), 1px gold border (`#cfa758`), 4px corner radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Label rendered in uppercase with subtle tracking. Hover state transitions to tertiary emerald (`#083c28`) with an intensified gold glow.
- **Secondary Champagne Action**: Gold outline (`#cfa758`, 1.5px), transparent background, charcoal text (`#111827`) or cream text when placed on dark surfaces. Hover shifts to a 10% champagne wash.
- **Text Link**: Charcoal with a subtle bottom hairline border in `#cfa758`, transitioning on hover with an animated expansion of the rule.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- **Event Chips (Dates, Venue, Tier)**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`), background in `cream-surface`, bordered by `border-refined` (1px). Text set in `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Gold Royal Badges**: Champagne gold subtle gradient fill with deep emerald text, accompanied by a miniature star or mallets vector icon.

### Luxury Cards (Tournament Cups & Strategic Pillars)
- **Tournament Cup Cards (Royal Cup, Polo Queens Match, All-Stars)**: Layered cream background (`#f7f5ed`), bordered by a double hairline accent or 1px `border-warm`. Includes an embossed trophy icon or crest placeholder, `headline-sm` title, and gold label badge. Hover state elevates the card subtly with a gentle champagne border transition.
- **Strategic Pillar Cards**: Clean 5-column or responsive modular grid. Pure minimal ivory containers with a gold geometric icon at the top, clear `title-md` header, and crisp body description.

### VIP Hospitality & Cultural Performance Modules
- Features dark emerald background panels (`#062d1d`), providing dramatic editorial contrast. Text switches to ivory and champagne gold accents. Subtle cream/gold divider lines separate schedules and culinary menus.

### Accordions (FAQ & Schedules)
- Clean horizontal separation using `1px solid #e5e0d3`. No background fills in resting state; subtle cream surface (`#f7f5ed`) on expanded state. Header typography uses `title-md`. The indicator toggle is an elegant slim gold plus/minus (`+` / `−`) or delicate chevron.

### Form Inputs
- Structured fields with cream background (`#f7f5ed`) and crisp `1px` border in `#dcd5c2`. Focus state shifts the border to champagne gold (`#cfa758`) with zero heavy outer glow, preserving crisp tailoring. Labels set in `label-md`.

### Registration CTA Banner
- High-impact section anchored by deep emerald green or rich ivory with a framed gold border enclosure. Features prominent display typography, succinct hospitality perks, and an expansive primary button flanked by security and prestige guarantees.
